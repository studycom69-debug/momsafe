Maternal Health Technology Platform

About MomSafe AI

MomSafe AI is a maternal health technology platform developed by MateraCare Technologies Private Limited. It is designed to support pregnant women with accessible digital health tools, personalized pregnancy support, health monitoring, AI-assisted insights, and connected care.

Built by MateraCare Technologies Private Limited AI-Assisted Maternal Insights Continuous Care Coordination

Our Mission

Our mission is to improve continuity of maternal care between healthcare visits by helping mothers better understand their health information, track important pregnancy-related data, and access appropriate support when needed.

Continuity of Care

Bridging communication and awareness between routine prenatal checkups so expectant mothers never feel isolated.

Maternal Empowerment

Equipping mothers with intuitive explanations and contextual insights regarding their everyday vital trends.

Timely Awareness

Providing structured tracking tools that encourage early contact with healthcare providers when changes occur.

What We Are Building

MomSafe AI is building a connected maternal-care ecosystem designed to bring together software, sensor data, and intelligent assistance to create a unified companion for pregnancy journeys:

AI-Powered Maternal Assistance

Intelligent conversational guidance and educational answers tailored to pregnancy questions and trimester-specific context.

Health & Symptom Tracking

Structured daily logs for blood pressure, heart rate, blood oxygen, temperature, fetal kicks, symptoms, and mood.

Personalized Pregnancy Support

Gestational age-adapted insights, milestones, checkup reminders, and stage-specific wellness guidance.

Nutrition & Medication Support

Hydration goals, maternal nutrition tracking, and adherence schedules for prescribed prenatal vitamins and medications.

Wearable-Based Health Monitoring

Integration with biometric sensor hardware and wearables for continuous visibility into maternal physiological trends. Learn more →

Alerts & Risk-Support Tools

Intelligent thresholds to identify out-of-range patterns and prompt prompt communication with care teams.

Hospital & Healthcare-Provider Connectivity

Location-aware healthcare facility navigation, directory access, and exportable health summaries to streamline consultations with doctors and clinics.

Important Clinical Transparency: MomSafe AI features are built for personal health tracking, supportive education, and communication coordination. None of the tools are intended to replace a certified physician, obstetrician, or clinical diagnostic equipment.

Our Approach

MomSafe AI aims to combine software, AI, wearable health data, maternal-care workflows, and accessible communication channels to help improve maternal health support and continuity of care.

1

Software

Intuitive, high-reliability web and mobile applications designed to make tracking and accessing maternal records effortless and clear.

2

3

4

Maternal-Care Workflows

Multi-channel connectivity ensuring mothers, caregivers, and emergency contacts stay informed and synchronized.

Responsible Use

MomSafe AI is designed to support maternal health awareness and care coordination. It is not intended to replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or emergency medical services.

If you are experiencing severe symptoms, vaginal bleeding, acute pelvic pain, reduced fetal movements, fluid leakage, or any emergency medical concern, please immediately contact your physician, call emergency medical services, or visit the nearest hospital emergency room.
