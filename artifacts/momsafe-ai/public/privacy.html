Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

MomSafe AI ("MomSafe", "we", "us", or "our") operates the MomSafe AI web application and related services (collectively, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information, including sensitive personal and health-related data, when you use our Service. Please read this policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access the Service.

1. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information from and about users of our Service, including the following:

1.1 Personal Information You Provide

  • Account and profile data: full name, email address, gestational age or due date, pregnancy-related profile information, and any information you provide during onboarding.
  • Authentication data: when you sign in with Google or other OAuth providers, we receive your display name, email address, and a unique user identifier.
  • User-generated content: health logs, daily journal entries, medication records, nutrition entries, mood inputs, and any notes or files you upload.
  • Communications: messages, support requests, and feedback you send to us.

1.2 Health and Wellness Data

  • Vitals and biometrics: heart rate, blood pressure, blood oxygen, weight, temperature, glucose, kick counts, sleep duration and quality, and similar measurements you enter manually or that are transmitted from connected devices (such as ESP32 sensors or wearables).
  • Nutrition data: meals logged, calories, macronutrients, hydration, caffeine intake, and dietary restrictions.
  • Medication and supplement data: medication names, dosages, frequency, adherence records, and prescriptions where provided.
  • Location information: approximate location when you use the "nearby hospitals" or location-based features of the Service.

1.3 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage data: browser type, device information, IP address, pages visited, time spent on features, referring/exit pages, and clickstream data.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: we use local storage, session tokens, and analytics cookies (e.g., Vercel Analytics) to deliver functionality and understand aggregate usage patterns.
  • Performance data: error reports, crash logs, and telemetry used to diagnose issues and improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to operate, maintain, improve, and develop the Service. Specifically, we use your information for the following purposes:

  • To provide the Service: create and manage your account, authenticate you, deliver dashboards, and maintain your health records.
  • AI guidance and personalized insights: analyze health inputs with our risk and guidance engines to surface personalized tips, alerts, and educational content. These outputs are informational only and do not constitute medical advice.
  • Risk alerts and notifications: detect out-of-range vitals, medication reminders, appointment prompts, and emergency contact escalations where configured.
  • Analytics and product improvement: perform internal analytics to understand feature usage, troubleshoot bugs, and design new capabilities. Aggregated or de-identified data may be used for research purposes.
  • Communications: send service announcements, security notices, and response to support inquiries. We may send marketing emails only with your explicit consent, and you can opt out at any time.
  • Legal compliance, safety, and enforcement: comply with applicable laws, regulations, and lawful requests; protect the safety, rights, and property of MomSafe, our users, and the public; and detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent, abusive, or unlawful activity.
  • With your consent: for any other purpose disclosed at the point of collection or authorized by you in writing.

We share data with vendors and subcontractors that perform services on our behalf, including hosting (Supabase, Vercel), infrastructure, analytics, and email delivery providers. Each provider is bound by contractual obligations to protect the confidentiality and security of your data.

When you choose to register or sign in to MomSafe AI using your Google Account ("Google Sign-In"), we request access only to your basic account information (name, email address, and profile picture) to authenticate you and create your account.

  • Purpose of use: Your Google user data is used exclusively to verify your identity, maintain your account profile, and deliver service-related notices.
  • No sale or advertising: We never sell, rent, or license your Google user data. We do not use or transfer Google user data for serving personalized, retargeted, or interest-based advertising.

6. Data Security

The security of your personal and health information is important to us. We implement commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect against unauthorized access, use, alteration, or disclosure, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256) via our infrastructure providers.
  • Role-based access controls and least-privilege principles for internal systems.
  • Automated monitoring for anomalous activity, abuse, and security incidents.
  • Regular security assessments and vulnerability management.

No method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you have reason to believe your interaction with us is no longer secure, please notify us immediately at the contact address below.
