Hospital-Deployed Solution

The MomSafe Pregnancy Monitoring Kit

Designed as a comprehensive B2B solution for hospitals and maternal-care providers. The MomSafe Kit equips mothers with connected devices and software to maintain visibility across the prenatal journey.

Component A

MomSafe Connected Wearable

MomSafe AI has developed a lightweight, non-invasive maternal wearable worn comfortably day and night to capture continuous physiological trend data between routine prenatal checkups.

Monitored Physiological Indicators:
  • Heart Rate — resting pulse trends
  • SpO₂ — peripheral blood oxygen saturation
  • Skin Temperature — continuous thermal stability
  • HRV — autonomic nervous response tracking
  • Sleep Patterns — nocturnal rest intervals
  • Daily Activity — gentle movement & step pacing
Tracks ongoing baseline variations passively without interrupting maternal daily routines.
Component B

MomSafe Smart Blood Pressure Monitor

Blood pressure is measured using a dedicated smart connected blood pressure device included in the MomSafe Kit, entirely distinct and separate from wearable sensing.

Dedicated BP Architecture:
  • Separate Dedicated Device: Blood pressure is NOT measured by the wearable device.
  • Standardized Arm Cuff: Mother takes spot-check BP readings using the dedicated smart BP device.
  • Seamless Synchronization: Readings synchronize directly into the MomSafe platform app.
  • Longitudinal BP History: Forms a clean clinical timeline for obstetric consultations.
Important Distinction: MomSafe does not make cuffless BP claims; blood pressure is monitored exclusively with the separate smart BP device.
Component C

MomSafe Digital Platform

A centralized maternal application and hospital coordination interface connecting device telemetry with self-reported health context.

Platform Capabilities:
  • Maternal Dashboard — intuitive daily overview
  • Symptom & Daily Logs — structured self-reporting
  • Medication & Nutrition — adherence tracking
  • Pregnancy Educational Library — stage-matched context
  • AI-Assisted Summaries — longitudinal pattern review
  • Hospital & Provider Link — exportable reports
Provides a shared, structured record accessible during clinical consultations.
Operational Workflow

How the Monitoring Flow Works

From hospital enrollment to prenatal consultations, MomSafe creates an organized continuum of care without adding complex burdens to mothers or clinical staff.

1 Enrollment

Hospital Enrolls Mother

Healthcare providers register the expectant mother in the hospital maternal management system during an initial prenatal checkup.

2 Kit Deployment

MomSafe Kit Provided

Mother receives the complete MomSafe Kit containing the wearable sensor, smart blood pressure monitor, and digital mobile access.

3 Continuous Data

Wearable Collects Trends

The wearable passively records pulse patterns, blood oxygen, temperature variation, HRV, sleep, and physical activity across day and night.

4 BP Protocol

Smart BP Monitor Checks

The mother performs scheduled blood pressure checks using the dedicated smart BP monitor, synchronizing records directly into the platform.

5 Lifestyle Logs

Maternal Daily Logs

Mother records daily observations such as symptoms, prenatal supplements, nutrition, hydration levels, and contextual well-being notes.

6 Organization

Platform Organizes Data

MomSafe aggregates wearable data, BP measurements, and symptom logs into structured longitudinal timelines and visual trends.

7 AI Context

AI Summarizes Patterns

Assistive algorithms summarize trends and highlight variations over time, offering supportive educational context without diagnostic interpretation.

8 Clinical Review

Care Team Follow-Up

During hospital visits or telehealth consultations, obstetric teams review organized longitudinal summaries to guide personalized care discussions.

Important Clinical Boundary: The MomSafe workflow facilitates information organization and care continuity. It does not automatically diagnose pregnancy complications or replace routine clinical assessments.

Care Continuity Model

Supporting Continuity Between Prenatal Visits

Traditional prenatal care relies on scheduled periodic visits. However, maternal physiological adaptations and symptoms develop on a continuous daily basis between these appointments.

MomSafe bridges this gap by providing an organized repository of daily observations, ensuring that changes over weeks or months are systematically documented rather than lost to recall bias.

✓

✓
Organized Patient Dialogue: Mothers attend visits with structured data, enabling more productive consultations.
✓
Healthcare Team Visibility: Equips obstetricians and midwives with verifiable trend summaries between scheduled appointments.
Prenatal Care Model Comparison
Traditional Periodic Care Intermittent

Vital signs recorded only at monthly or bi-weekly clinic visits. Days in between lack verifiable tracking, relying heavily on maternal retrospective memory.

MomSafe Connected Monitoring Continuous

Wearable tracks passive baseline variations 24/7; smart BP captures spot checks; digital platform structures logs into unified records reviewed by hospital teams.

Designed to enhance clinical continuity, not replace formal prenatal examinations or obstetric visits.

Safety & Compliance Standards

Responsible Use & Medical Boundaries

Primary Purpose Statement:

“MomSafe AI is designed to support maternal health monitoring, information organization, and care continuity. It is not intended to replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency medical services.”

MomSafe AI provides supportive informational technology to assist mothers in understanding trends and to help hospitals coordinate longitudinal maternal data. The platform and its connected devices do not provide medical diagnosis, predict acute obstetric emergencies, or prescribe pharmacological therapies.

Critical Emergency Guidance

If you experience severe symptoms — including severe headache, visual disturbances, vaginal bleeding, acute abdominal pain, sudden severe swelling, or reduced fetal movement — do not rely on app summaries. Seek immediate emergency medical care or proceed to your nearest maternity hospital immediately.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about MomSafe AI, hospital deployment, hardware sensors, and clinical boundaries.

What is pregnancy monitoring with MomSafe AI?

Pregnancy monitoring with MomSafe AI is a hospital-connected maternal health solution that combines non-invasive connected devices (a maternal wearable and a smart blood pressure monitor) with a digital health platform and assistive intelligence to help mothers and clinical teams maintain continuous care visibility between routine prenatal checkups.

What does the MomSafe Kit include?

The MomSafe Kit deployed by hospitals includes: (1) the MomSafe connected maternal health wearable, (2) the MomSafe smart blood pressure monitor, (3) access to the MomSafe maternal digital platform with daily symptom and medication logs, (4) educational health tracking tools, and (5) secure provider connectivity for clinical follow-up.

Does the MomSafe wearable measure blood pressure?

No. The MomSafe wearable does not measure blood pressure. Blood pressure is measured exclusively using the separate MomSafe smart blood pressure monitoring device included in the MomSafe Kit. Readings from this smart BP device synchronize directly into the MomSafe platform to build a longitudinal record.

What health indicators does the MomSafe wearable track?

The MomSafe wearable passively tracks six core maternal physiological indicators: resting heart rate, blood oxygen saturation (SpO₂), skin temperature trends, heart rate variability (HRV), nocturnal sleep intervals, and daily physical activity pacing.

How is AI used in MomSafe?

Artificial intelligence operates strictly as an assistive intelligence layer within MomSafe. It synthesizes large amounts of longitudinal data (wearable signals, BP readings, symptoms, and lifestyle logs) into organized trend summaries and stage-appropriate educational context, making data easier for mothers to understand and for healthcare teams to review.

Does MomSafe AI diagnose pregnancy complications?

No. MomSafe AI does not diagnose pregnancy complications, detect medical pathologies, or prescribe clinical treatments. Diagnosis and clinical interpretation remain solely with qualified medical practitioners. MomSafe is an informational and tracking platform designed to support informed discussions.

Is MomSafe designed for hospitals?

Yes. MomSafe AI is primarily designed as a B2B maternal-health platform for hospitals, obstetric clinics, and healthcare systems seeking to extend structured maternal care coordination, improve longitudinal trend visibility, and enhance patient engagement beyond physical clinical appointments.

How can MomSafe help between prenatal visits?

Between prenatal appointments, MomSafe continuously captures physiological patterns, tracks regular smart BP measurements, and records daily symptoms and habits. This replaces incomplete recollections with an organized, chronological summary that obstetricians can quickly review during routine visits.
