Care Continuity

Bridging the 2–4 Week Gap Between Prenatal Visits

Standard prenatal care schedules clinic visits 2 to 4 weeks apart during the first two trimesters. In between visits, physiological baseline shifts occur outside the clinic walls.

MomSafe AI equips healthcare providers with continuous home data streams, ensuring clinicians start every conversation with complete longitudinal history rather than unassisted recall.

Clinical boundary: MomSafe AI is an asynchronous trend aggregation system, not an intensive 24/7 emergency surveillance monitor.

Clinical Ethics & Governance

Technology That Supports Care — Not Replaces It

MomSafe is designed with deep respect for the doctor-patient relationship. We organize data so physicians can practice medicine with better context.

MomSafe Supports:
  • • Organizing health information: Aggregating wearable vitals, BP readings, and symptom entries continuously.
  • • Summarizing trends: Presenting baseline shifts and week-over-week trends clearly.
  • • Preparing consultations: Providing structured summaries before scheduled hospital visits.
  • • Supporting continuity: Keeping mothers engaged and reassured between appointments.
Physicians Remain Responsible For:
  • • Clinical Diagnosis: Evaluating preeclampsia, hypertension, or gestational conditions.
  • • Treatment & Prescriptions: Deciding medication, clinical interventions, and delivery plans.
  • • Emergency Decisions: Assessing urgent clinical symptoms and emergency triage.
  • • Direct Care: Providing hands-on clinical examinations and compassionate bedside care.

Hospital FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does MomSafe integrate with existing Hospital EHR systems?
MomSafe supports standards-based FHIR and HL7 data exchange for patient vitals and PDF clinical summary generation. Clinicians can review data through the MomSafe Hospital Web Portal or export standardized reports directly into their electronic health records.
Why is blood pressure measured with a separate upper-arm monitor?
Accurate maternal blood pressure monitoring requires standard oscillometric upper-arm occlusion. Optical wrist approximations can be imprecise during pregnancy due to systemic vascular resistance shifts. MomSafe pairs continuous wrist telemetry for passive metrics with a dedicated upper-arm cuff for blood pressure.
Does MomSafe require our staff to monitor alerts 24/7?
No. MomSafe is explicitly built as an asynchronous trend aggregation system, not an intensive care unit telemetry monitor. It does not place a 24/7 emergency response burden on hospital staff. It organizes data to be reviewed prior to or during scheduled consultations.
How is patient data secured and protected?
All patient telemetry and health records are encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256 encryption. We implement strict role-based access control, ensuring only authorized care-team members assigned to a patient can view her data.
