Prenatal Care Continuity

Extending Structured Visibility Between Scheduled Visits

Standard prenatal care inherently depends on episodic hospital consultations occurring weeks or months apart. However, key physiological variations, daily symptoms, and maternal health changes unfold continuously between those appointments.

MomSafe AI is being designed to give hospitals a structured, connected approach to bridge this gap—giving clinical teams organized visibility into home-based trends without implying continuous clinical surveillance or replacing patient check-ins.

Standard Care Routine

Spot measurements captured every 2–4 weeks during brief clinic visits. Daily changes, fluctuations between check-ups, and home blood pressure variations often remain unrecorded.

With MomSafe AI

Passive background trends from wearables, home blood-pressure readings from a smart upper-arm cuff, and digital symptom logs organized into clear summaries for visits.

Designed to support doctor-patient conversations with organized contextual history rather than replace clinical decision-making.
The MomSafe Ecosystem

What Hospitals Get With MomSafe AI

A coherent multi-layered architecture combining specialized hardware, digital tracking, and assistive intelligence designed around maternal wellbeing.

01 • Hardware Sensor Layer

Connected Maternal Wearable

Passive sensor data captured throughout the day and night to establish baseline personal trends across key physiological parameters:

  • Heart Rate: Heart-rate trends throughout the day and night
  • Blood Oxygen (SpO₂): Overnight and daytime oxygenation levels
  • Skin Temperature: Skin-temperature trends across gestational weeks
  • HRV & Sleep: Heart rate variability trends and sleep
  • Activity: Movement patterns and daily activity tracking
Hardware Note: The wearable tracks biometrics such as heart rate and temperature. It does not measure blood pressure.
02 • Dedicated Cardiovascular Tracking

Smart Blood-Pressure Monitor

A separate smart upper-arm blood-pressure monitor dedicated to capturing reliable cardiovascular readings between hospital visits:

  • Systolic Blood Pressure: Recorded via standardized upper-arm cuff measurements
  • Diastolic Blood Pressure: Consistent logging across gestational weeks
  • Systolic & Diastolic Readings: Automatically synced to patient profile
  • Organized BP Records: Easy charting for gestational review
Distinct Device: Blood pressure is tracked solely using this separate upper-arm monitor, avoiding wrist-based approximations.
03 • Patient Engagement & Logging

Digital Maternal-Health Platform

A digital platform built for expectant mothers to record daily qualitative observations and organize pregnancy health information:

  • Symptom Tracking: Nausea, fatigue, swelling, kicks, and headache logs
  • Nutrition Support: Nutrition logs and prenatal supplement records
  • Medication Support: Prescribed medication logs and reminders
  • Pregnancy Context: Educational resources and timeline milestones
Longitudinal Context: Creates an organized timeline connecting subjective symptoms with physiological sensor readings.
04 • Synthesis & Structuring

Assistive AI Layer

An intelligent assistant designed to synthesize high-volume personal data into clear, understandable summaries:

  • Pattern Structuring: Groups daily data into structured chronological records
  • Trend Summarization: Summarizes weekly vitals for quick clinician review
  • Visit Preparation: Organizes questions and symptoms ahead of check-ups
  • Communication Support: Helps mothers articulate changes clearly
Strict Non-Diagnostic Role: The AI never diagnoses diseases or prescribes care. Clinical decisions remain the sole responsibility of doctors.

Physician-Centric Philosophy

Technology That Supports Care — Not Replaces It

MomSafe AI is designed strictly as an assistive layer around healthcare providers. We believe the relationship between a mother and her obstetrician is irreplaceable.

What MomSafe AI Does Not Do
  • • It does not diagnose medical conditions.
  • • It does not prescribe treatments or adjust medications.
  • • It does not replace obstetricians or midwives.
  • • It does not make clinical decisions independently.
What MomSafe AI Does
  • • It organizes multi-week health data systematically.
  • • It summarizes physiological trends for rapid review.
  • • It empowers mothers with clear personal wellness visibility.
  • • It supports care continuity between clinical visits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Key questions regarding MomSafe AI's hospital integration and device ecosystem.

What is MomSafe AI for hospitals?
MomSafe AI is a connected maternal-care platform designed for hospitals, combining wearable health monitoring, smart blood-pressure tracking, digital logging, and an assistive AI layer to help care teams maintain structured continuity between scheduled prenatal visits.
What does the MomSafe wearable track?
The maternal wearable tracks heart rate, blood oxygen saturation (SpO₂), skin-temperature trends, heart rate variability (HRV), sleep, and activity.
Does the MomSafe wearable measure blood pressure?
No. The MomSafe wearable does not measure blood pressure. Blood pressure is tracked using a completely separate smart upper-arm blood-pressure monitor included in the MomSafe Kit.
How is blood pressure recorded in MomSafe?
Expectant mothers take blood pressure readings at home using the separate smart upper-arm blood-pressure monitor. Measurements (systolic and diastolic blood-pressure readings) sync with the MomSafe digital platform.
Does MomSafe AI replace a doctor?
Never. MomSafe AI is an assistive technology layer designed to support healthcare providers, not replace them. Obstetricians and clinical teams remain fully central and responsible for all clinical assessments, diagnoses, and medical decisions.
How can hospitals use MomSafe between prenatal visits?
Hospitals can offer MomSafe as part of an institutional prenatal care package. Between visits, the platform captures wearable baselines, blood pressure readings, and patient logs, organizing them into clear summaries that doctors can review at scheduled consultations.
Is MomSafe AI a diagnostic platform?
No. MomSafe AI is not a diagnostic device and does not provide automated diagnoses, clinical prescriptions, or real-time emergency triage. It is designed to summarize patterns and organize health information to assist qualified clinicians.
